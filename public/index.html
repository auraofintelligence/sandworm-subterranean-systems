Wet sandExclusive initial geological research domain

Sandworm is an independent proposal to develop specialist machinery, materials and digital systems for constructing and expanding underground infrastructure in saturated silica and heavy mineral sands. Start with useful public services and local skills, not a promise of an instant subterranean city.

The governing idea

Existing engineering is an evidence library, not the limit of human imagination. Neither a textbook nor an attractive rendering is proof of what can or cannot be built. Every proposed departure earns its place through measurement, replication, risk control and independently reviewable evidence.
