Chapter 15 / 17 · Development pathway

Start with a laboratory. Earn the next scale.

A staged venture development plan that ties every claim to an experiment, a cost record and a decision.

Development gates

GateWorkDecision evidence
0: definePublic needs, comparative costs, source ledger, stakeholders, hazard registerScope and comparable service outcomes
1: characteriseLawfully obtained samples, mineral and water characterisation, baseline modelsRepeatable properties and uncertainty
2: bench researchNon-occupied contained tests, material coupons, sensors, simulationDocumented performance and failures
3: controlled demonstratorApproved contained test facility and independent reviewSafety, containment and economics evidence
4: pilot business caseSite investigations, options appraisal, owner engagement, procurement pathwayIndependent engineering and cost assurance
5: scale only if warrantedAuthorised service project, operational capability and lifecycle fundingDemonstrated benefits and required approvals

Make learning compound

Each experiment produces an evidence bundle: question, assumptions, model, materials, calibration, results, failures, reproducibility notes, cost and decision. Local compute, training and fabrication should continue to generate value even if a particular underground concept fails.

Country, consent and statutory pathways

Early engagement with Quandamooka Traditional Owners and relevant land, water, marine, planning, environmental, workplace safety, utility and transport authorities is essential. Establish legal authority before site activities. Do not imply endorsement, access rights or permission from any party. Commission suitably qualified independent professionals for regulated design and assurance.

The longer view

Civilisation of Sand ↗ holds the wider speculative horizon. The immediate Sandworm business proposition is narrower: validated wet-sand engineering, service infrastructure, material utilisation, distributed compute and local capability. Scale follows results, not a predetermined city blueprint.

Marine robotics and space analogues

Apply this research stream to robotic aquaculture and modular construction, with separate marine costs, permissions, environmental limits and off-world qualification requirements.

Keep the productive wealth local

Explore shared ownership of Sandworm and island community sovereign wealth funds, linking Mutual Futures with long-term local reinvestment and community decision rights.

The first practical research target

Begin with the requirements for robot-serviceable utility tunnels in an approximately 1 to 1.5 metre diameter research range. Establish how much usable space remains when utilities and structural systems are included, what servicing tasks are required and what happens when a robot or utility fails. Develop experimental test environments before committing to any site, machinery or construction specification.

Small experiments, frontier ambitions

The purpose of staged testing is to build increasingly capable autonomous machinery, novel materials and integrated infrastructure, not to restrict the vision to familiar technology. Measure and iterate from first principles.

Gumpi Town Tunnel Loop

Prioritise the Gumpi Town Tunnel Loop as the first proposed Sandworm infrastructure project on Minjerribah. Establish parking and mobility demand, actual site data, technology research and the alternative business case before final engineering or construction. Explore the first-project proposal.

An integrated first-project research programme

Build the Gumpi case around mobility, both ferry terminals, material recovery, robotic fabrication, local energy and storage research. Use the Clean Energy Superpower ledger and Gumpi Lab spatial baseline to compare coupled scenarios before selecting designs.
