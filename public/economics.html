Chapter 14 / 17 · Cost workbench

Make the alternative earn its numbers

Compare complete service systems across the same scope, service population and lifecycle. All new-project values are user-entered scenarios.

Capital and lifecycle scenario

Use the exact capital principal from the Rendalls SMBI scenario as an editable comparison reference. It is not an official budget. Enter independently developed costs for the Sandworm alternative; blank fields prevent false precision.

Annual financing is calculated on end-of-year payments, unlike the cited paper's beginning-of-year assumption. Grant share reduces borrowing only. The annual operations allowance should include renewal provisioning if desired; the tool does not model depreciation as an additional cash expense. This is not a discounted cash-flow appraisal, household tariff or quote.

Evidence needed to replace placeholders

Route quantities

Service lengths, geotechnical classes, depth constraints, crossings, access and reinstatement.

Process economics

Utilisation, excavation productivity, downtime, consumables, separation, disposal and energy.

Lifecycle costs

Service reliability, access, pump replacement, inspections, maintenance, renewals and decommissioning.

Sensitivity

Run low, base and high cases; hold service coverage and environmental performance constant; quantify risk correlations.

Do not double count

Separate the sewerage reference from the broader infrastructure backlog, separate terminal funding from tunnel research, and credit verified net material revenue only once. Shared corridor costs must be allocated across beneficiaries without charging the same asset twice.

Marine robotics and space analogues

Apply this research stream to robotic aquaculture and modular construction, with separate marine costs, permissions, environmental limits and off-world qualification requirements.

Modular solar roads and paths

Explore interlocking paving for roads, walking paths and cycleways as a potential recovered-sand manufacturing, stormwater, energy and autonomous maintenance research programme.

Keep the productive wealth local

Explore shared ownership of Sandworm and island community sovereign wealth funds, linking Mutual Futures with long-term local reinvestment and community decision rights.

Compare accessible corridors with single-service installations

Use the initial 1 to 1.5 metre diameter corridor concept as a research scenario, not a supplier quote. Separate excavation, finished lining, service installation, isolation, access, robotic maintenance systems, recovery, inspections, renewals and end-of-life costs. Compare equivalent coverage and service performance across conventional separate pipes and cables, and robot-serviceable tunnels. An initial capital premium could be justified only by demonstrated lifecycle benefits. Do not use the nominal underwater pipeline cost per metre as a tunnel construction rate.
