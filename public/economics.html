How the calculator works

The tool uses end-of-year loan repayments, unlike the reference paper's beginning-of-year assumption. Grants reduce borrowing. Include expected operating and renewal costs, and deduct only verified net income. It is an illustrative comparison, not a rate, quote or full discounted cash-flow analysis.

Reference assumptions ↗

Don't just compare the price of a hole

Build

Dig, line, fit out and connect.

Operate

Pay for power, servicing and people.

Repair

Inspect, replace parts and renew equipment.

Recover

Reuse useful material and account for end-of-life costs.

A conventional pipe, a cable and a robot-accessible tunnel do different jobs. Compare equivalent service coverage, reliability and useful life. A higher construction cost could make sense if the lifetime benefits are demonstrated.
