Core posture

Self-sovereign means people keep the steering wheel.

The site does not ask visitors to believe everything, join everything or hand over private data. It offers trails, asks better questions and helps create useful notes.

What can we learn, build, map, repair or film together while keeping consent, dignity and local authority intact?
