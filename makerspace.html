Grounded start

What can a modern ferry gateway teach before anyone talks about tunnels?

Dunwich / Gumpi is already a living transport system: vehicle ferries, passenger services, buses, turnarounds, freight, visitors and daily local movement. Could that make it a strong first place for open data, public evidence, maker-space tools and tunnel questions?

A good first lab helps people understand the place before it asks them to believe a plan.
