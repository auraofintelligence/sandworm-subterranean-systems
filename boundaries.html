Boundaries Keep The Work Human

Consent, data dignity, cultural authority, safety and source posture for the Sandworm trail.

Human guardrails

Boundaries make the exploration stronger.

They keep the work from sliding into extraction, false authority, private-data capture or unsafe engineering momentum.

Consent

Ask before carrying someone else's story

A story, image, place memory or cultural detail can be powerful without being yours to publish.

Data

Keep private layers private

A digital twin should clarify public systems without exposing household, health, identity or vulnerable-person details.

Culture

Do not claim authority you do not hold

Public pages should not imply endorsement, approval or representation where it has not been granted.

Safety

Prototype behind gates

Material, tunnel, energy, sensor and machine ideas need review before they become physical activity.

Sources

Questions before claims

Speculative notes can inspire better questions without becoming public certainty.

Sovereignty

Participation stays voluntary

People should be able to use, reject, correct or ignore the tools without losing dignity.
