Story as inspection

The fiction can carry the possibility. The documentary can carry the receipts.

Sandworm is not only an engineering idea. It is a way to ask whether a community can turn difficult infrastructure into story, learning, review, consent, wealth and ecological repair.

A good story trail does not take the story. It makes the next respectful conversation easier.

Fiction

Imagine the solved problems

A future story can show tunnels, reefs, wildlife crossings, sand batteries and public ledgers as lived choices, not slogans.

Documentary

Follow the first attempts

The camera can stay with source trails, makers, sceptics, reviewers, failed tests and careful improvements.

Festival

Give people entry points

Screenings, builder forms and discussion trails let people explore without being told what to think.
