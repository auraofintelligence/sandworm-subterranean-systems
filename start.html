Pick your entry

You do not need to start with the biggest idea.

A useful trail is simply a sequence of questions that leaves you with more clarity and more agency than you had before.

  1. Touch the surface

    Start with a maker-space question: what could be repaired, prototyped, measured or taught locally?

  2. Map the shared place

    Move into digital twin questions: what is public, what needs permission and what should stay private?

  3. Ask the subterranean question

    Explore Sandworm systems as cautious prototypes, simulations and source trails before any physical ambition.

  4. Open the simulation layer

    Use Civilisation of Sand as a story-space for capability, resource loops and choices under pressure.

  5. Carry it through culture

    When the idea needs people, memory and public meaning, bring in film and documentary builders.

Leave with a Markdown handoff.

A clean `.md` draft is a small bridge between high-level thought and practical next work.
